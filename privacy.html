Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

About this policy

Bozsoft is an independent software and game development studio. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, applications, websites, and related services developed, published, or operated by Bozsoft (collectively, our "Services").

We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is processed when you use our Services.

Not all Services use the same features or third-party integrations. Certain practices described below apply only when the relevant functionality is available in a particular application or version.

Information processed by our Services

Depending on the application and its features, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Gameplay and application data: Game progress, scores, achievements, unlocked content, preferences, and settings.
  • Device and technical information: Device type, operating system, application version, diagnostic information, and technical information necessary to operate or troubleshoot our Services.
  • Online service information: Network requests and related technical information processed when an application connects to online configuration, leaderboards, or other connected services.
  • Advertising information: Information processed by advertising providers, only in applications that include advertising functionality.
  • Communications: Your email address and the information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

The specific information processed depends on the application, its configuration, the features you use, and any third-party services involved. This list does not mean that every Bozsoft application collects every category of information.

Local application data

Our applications may store gameplay progress, high scores, settings, and other preferences locally on your device. This information helps maintain your progress and preferred application settings.

Locally stored information generally remains on your device unless you delete it, reset the application, or uninstall it. Device backup systems may preserve copies depending on your settings.

Some applications may also use online services. Where applicable, additional information may be processed by those services as described in this policy.

Game Center and platform services

Some of our games may integrate with Apple Game Center or similar platform services to provide leaderboards, achievements, or other connected features.

When you use Apple Game Center, Apple processes relevant account and gameplay information according to its own privacy practices. Depending on your Game Center settings, your nickname, achievements, or leaderboard scores may be visible to other players.

You can manage Game Center through your Apple device settings. For more information, see Apple's Game Center privacy information.

Applications without these integrations do not use them.

Advertising and third-party advertising services

Some Bozsoft applications may display advertisements using third-party advertising providers, such as Google AdMob. Other applications may contain no advertising at all.

Where advertising is enabled, advertising providers may process information such as IP addresses, device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on their SDKs, configurations, applicable laws, and your privacy choices.

Such information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services.

Where required, personalized advertising or tracking is subject to applicable consent and permission requirements.

You can manage application tracking permissions through your device's privacy settings. Where an application offers advertising privacy controls, you may also manage applicable choices within that application.

Declining tracking permission does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Online connectivity and configuration

Some applications may connect to servers or online services to retrieve application configuration, provide connected features, or maintain service functionality.

These connections may involve technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, requested resources, and device or application information transmitted as part of normal network communication.

Service providers may process this information to deliver, secure, and maintain the relevant services. The availability of offline functionality varies by application.

Our website

Our website provides information about Bozsoft, our applications, and ways to contact us.

Our website does not currently provide user account registration, contact forms, or advertising and analytics scripts. Its own page code does not intentionally set cookies or use browser storage.

Our hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, requested pages, and request timestamps, to deliver and protect the website.

If our website functionality changes, we will update this policy as appropriate.

When you contact us

If you contact Bozsoft by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, investigate reported problems, and manage related communications.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless necessary for your request.

How information is used

Depending on the Service and the information involved, information may be processed to:

  • Operate and maintain our applications and websites.
  • Save gameplay progress and user preferences.
  • Provide optional connected features such as leaderboards.
  • Deliver advertising in applications that include advertisements.
  • Diagnose technical problems and protect service security.
  • Respond to support inquiries and privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services and external links

Our Services may integrate with third-party providers, including Apple, Google, hosting providers, and email service providers.

These providers may process information in connection with the services they deliver, subject to their respective privacy practices and applicable contractual arrangements.

When you follow an external link, such as an App Store listing or third-party website, the destination's privacy policy applies to your activity there.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect rights, security, and prevent abuse.

Data retention

We retain information under our control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing Services, responding to support requests, maintaining security, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the Service involved.

Information stored exclusively on your device generally remains there until you delete it or uninstall the relevant application, subject to device backup behavior.

Uninstalling an application does not automatically delete information held by third-party providers such as Apple Game Center or advertising services. Those providers apply their own retention and deletion practices.

Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request concerning information controlled by Bozsoft, contact bozsoftcontact@gmail.com.

We may need to verify your request before responding. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

You can manage device permissions through your operating system settings and use available in-app privacy controls. For information controlled independently by Apple, Google, or other providers, please use their privacy tools or contact them directly.

Children's privacy

Some Bozsoft applications may be suitable for younger audiences. The availability of child-directed features, advertising, and connected services depends on the individual application and applicable platform requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Bozsoft, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to relevant legal requirements and safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our Services, technology, legal requirements, or data practices.

When we make changes, we will publish the revised policy on this page and update the date shown above.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, our Services, or your privacy rights, contact Bozsoft at bozsoftcontact@gmail.com.

Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

About this policy

Bozsoft is an independent mobile game studio. This policy covers our mobile games, including Runaway Knight and Spin Frenzy, and our website. It explains the information processed when you play our games, use connected features, visit our website, or contact us. The features and services available vary by game and version.

Game information and connected features

Games may store progress, high scores, and preferences on your device to remember your settings and gameplay. Connected features may process gameplay information, such as scores and leaderboard entries, to provide those features.

Where Apple Game Center is available and you choose to use it, Apple processes your Game Center profile and related gameplay information to provide leaderboards and other gaming features. Your nickname and scores may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. You can manage Game Center in your device settings. See Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

Advertising

Games that include advertising may use Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded or interstitial ads. Depending on the SDK, configuration, and your privacy choices, Google’s advertising services may process IP addresses, device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These services use information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent requirements and your choices.

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking, and change advertising consent through in-game privacy controls where available. Declining tracking does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for more information.

Information on this website

The website currently has no account registration, contact forms, analytics scripts, or advertising scripts. Its own page code does not set cookies or use browser storage.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request times, to deliver and protect the website.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and handle related support requests. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Service providers and external links

Apple, Google, website hosting providers, and email providers may process information to deliver the services described above. Their own privacy policies explain their processing. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights, security, and prevent abuse. When you follow an external link, such as an App Store link, the destination’s privacy policy applies to your activity there.

Data retention

We retain information we control for as long as needed to provide the relevant service, handle support requests, and meet legal obligations. Information stored only on your device remains until you remove it or uninstall the game; device backups may retain copies. Uninstalling a game does not automatically delete records held by Game Center or advertising providers. Those providers apply their own retention and deletion policies.

Your privacy requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent. To make a request concerning information we control, email bozsoftcontact@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

You can withdraw permissions through your device settings and use available in-game privacy controls. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. For information controlled by Apple or Google, use their privacy settings or contact them directly.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Bozsoft, please contact us so we can investigate and address the request, including deleting information where appropriate.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, as described in their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

When this policy is updated, the revised version and its date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bozsoft at bozsoftcontact@gmail.com.
